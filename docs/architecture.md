# Architecture

## Product Pillars

### Mission
Planning, readiness, and drive/no-drive decisions.

### Fleet
Vehicles, fuel, insurance, maintenance, depreciation, and operating cost.

### Operations
Active shifts, trip decisions, platform strategy, and closeout.

### Business
Profit, taxes, reserves, goals, analytics, and forecasts.

## Code Organization

src/app
Routes and page entry points.

src/components
Reusable UI and domain components.

src/lib
Business logic, scoring, forecasting, finance, recommendations, analytics, and storage.

src/types
Shared domain types.

src/data
Mock data and seed fixtures.

docs
Product, design, architecture, and domain documentation.
